About

I'm Kevin Miller, the owner and woodworker behind Wooden Spoke, based in Eagle County, Colorado.

What started as a hobby has grown into a small woodworking business focused on custom carpentry and fine furniture. I build custom pieces with an emphasis on quality materials, thoughtful design, and craftsmanship that lasts.

I work on each project from design to delivery, whether it's a one-of-a-kind piece of furniture or a custom project for someone's home.

How I Work

Every project starts with a conversation about what you're looking for. From there, I'll work through the design, materials, and details to come up with something that fits your space and how you plan to use it.

I handle the building and finishing myself, with an emphasis on solid construction and getting the details right.

If you have something in mind, I'd love to hear about it.
